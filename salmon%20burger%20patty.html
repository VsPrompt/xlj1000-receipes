Salmon Burger Patties

Tender, savory salmon patties made with canned pink salmon, egg, and a little mayonnaise. Bake them until golden, then tuck into a bun or serve with a crisp salad.

Prep10 minutes
Bake15–20 minutes
Total25–30 minutes
Makes4–6 patties

Ingredients

Check off each item as you prepare it.

Texture tip: Drain the salmon well. Start with 2 tablespoons of cassava flour, then add more only if the mixture feels too wet to shape.

Instructions

1
Heat the oven

Preheat to 400°F (200°C). Lightly oil or line a baking sheet with parchment.

2
Prepare the salmon

Drain the canned salmon thoroughly and flake it in a medium bowl with a fork.

3
Mix the patties

Stir in the eggs, mayonnaise, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and pepper. Add cassava flour a tablespoon at a time until the mixture holds together. Taste before adding salt.

4
Shape

Form 4–6 evenly sized patties, about ¾ inch thick. Place them on the prepared baking sheet and gently flatten the tops.

5
Bake until set and golden

Bake for 15–20 minutes, turning carefully halfway through if you want both sides browned. Thicker patties may need a little longer; cook until the centers are hot and the patties hold together.

6
Rest and serve

Let the patties rest for 2 minutes. Serve in burger buns or lettuce wraps, or alongside vegetables and a squeeze of lemon if available.

Make-ahead: Shape the patties up to a day ahead, cover, and refrigerate. Handle gently when moving them to the baking sheet.
